Qur'an Studio Admin

Manage the data repo from your browser: app control, video backgrounds, reciters and builds.

GitHub connection

Use a fine-grained token for this repository only, with Contents: Read and write, and Actions: Read and write (to start builds).

App control

Edits data/app_config.json. Phones read it at every launch and when they return to the app (at most every 30 minutes). GitHub may keep the old copy about 5 minutes. No build is needed.

Announcement (shown once per id)

Maintenance

Switch off parts of the app

Add video backgrounds

MP4, about 10 to 15 s, 720p to 1080p, no sound, under 15 MB. Each clip needs a source and a licence or it is not published.

Published and pending clips

Reciter photos

Choose a photo for a reciter. It is cropped to a 300x300 square JPG automatically. The record needs a source and a licence, or the photo is not published.

Add a reciter from another source

Writes data/reciters_extra.json. The link must contain {SSS}{AAA} (surah and verse as three digits each, like EveryAyah) and the first verse is tested at build. Without source and licence it is skipped.

Reciters added here

Data build

A build starts by itself when you change reciter photos, extra reciters or video clips. Use this for anything else. After a build, open the app: More, Resources, Check for updates.

Last report

Templates

The 8 templates and their pictures are inside the app (assets/templates in the app repo), so they cannot be changed from here yet. Moving them to this repo needs a small app update; ask for it as the next batch.
